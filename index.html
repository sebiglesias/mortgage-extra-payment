Frequently Asked Questions

Mortgage interest is calculated monthly based on your remaining principal loan balance. By sending extra money designated specifically toward principal, you immediately lower the balance upon which all subsequent interest is computed, creating a snowball effect of savings.
Paying half your mortgage payment every two weeks results in 26 half-payments a year (equivalent to 13 full monthly payments instead of 12). That single extra payment each year automatically cuts 4 to 6 years off a standard 30-year fixed loan.
Paying off a 6.75% mortgage guarantees a risk-free 6.75% after-tax return on your money. If your mortgage rate is under 3.5%, investing in broad market index funds historically yields higher returns; however, at rates above 6%, paying down principal offers guaranteed, high-yield peace of mind.
